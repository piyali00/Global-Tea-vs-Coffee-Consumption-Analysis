# Global-Tea-vs-Coffee-Consumption-Analysis
To analyze global tea and coffee consumption patterns and understand the impact of:  Geography Income Age Lifestyle factors
📊 Global Tea vs Coffee Consumption Analysis
Tools Used: Python (Pandas), Excel, Data Visualization

🎯 Objective
1. To analyze global tea and coffee consumption patterns and understand the impact of:
2. Geography
3. Income
4. Age
5. Lifestyle factors

🔍 Key Insights
1. Tea and coffee consumption are globally balanced, with tea slightly dominating (~51%)
2. Tea is dominant in Asia, while coffee is preferred in other continents
3. Income level does not significantly impact beverage preference
4. No major difference found in lifestyle factors (sleep, stress, acidity)
5. Age groups show consistent preference, with slight dominance of tea

💡 Conclusion
Beverage preference is influenced more by regional factors than income, age, or lifestyle.
